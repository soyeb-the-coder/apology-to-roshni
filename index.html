To Roshni, With Regret & Love

🥀

Roshni,

I am writing this because I am really feeling very bad and ashamed of myself. I dont even know how to write this properly, but please read it once.

What I said to you that day in anger... calling you characterless... it was the biggest mistake of my life. I swear I feel so disgusting about myself for using that dirty word for you. You are my girl, and instead of respecting you and protecting you, I insulted your dignity. There is no excuse for what I said. Even if I was angry, how could I say that to you? You never deserved that, you are so pure and innocent, and I hurt you like an idiot.

"Next day when I saw you in school during teachers day, you were looking happy from behind... and like a coward I didn't even call you or came in front of you. I acted like nothing happened, talking normal, while you were carrying that deep pain inside your heart."

When you cried on call... it broke me completely. And knowing you were sick, alone in a dark room crying because of me, and I wasn't there to hold you or comfort you... I feel like the worst boyfriend in this world. You asked me "you don't care for me?" Roshni, that line is still hitting my chest. I care for you so much, but my actions made you feel alone when you needed me the most. I should have ran to you, I should have said sorry right then and there.

I don't have any right to force you to forgive me. My pride and foolishness gave you a scar. But please believe me, I will never, ever talk to you like this again or let my ego come before you. You mean everything to me. Take your time, get angry on me, scold me, but please don't cry alone like that. I am really, truly sorry Roshni.

Forever Grateful For You,

Yours, Always
